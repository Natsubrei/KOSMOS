# 0007 · Scenario and threshold model

## Question

How do conclusions derive from evidence: hardcoded diagnosis pipelines, a rule/data-driven scenario model, or a hybrid? Where do thresholds live (code, config, database), who can change them, and how does a scenario declare its required sources and degrade audibly when one is missing? Design against the report model so every derived conclusion carries its reasoning.

Blocked by: 0003 (conclusions and evidence must exist before their derivation is designed).
